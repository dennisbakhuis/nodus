# Manage

The manage area is where writers and admins configure the radar. Pick a section
from the sidebar; the Help button updates with guidance for it.

- **Cycles** — create, edit, and close assessment cycles.
- **Segments** *(admin)* — the slices of the radar and their themes.
- **Groups** — organise technologies into a multi-level taxonomy.
- **People** — the registry of owners and contributors.
- **Users** *(admin)* — application accounts and their roles.
- **Data Visibility** *(admin)* — which roles see which fields, and who can
  reach the list and cycle history.
- **Backup & Restore** *(admin)* — export and re-import the whole database.
- **Import References** — bring in peer references from another instance.
- **Settings** — branding, organisation details, and presentation mode.
- **API** — issue API keys and read the endpoint reference.

The whole area needs the **Writer** role; sections marked *(admin)* need
**Admin**.
