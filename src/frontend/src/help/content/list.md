# Technology list

The list shows the same technologies as the radar in a sortable, filterable
table — the fastest way to scan many at once, compare them, or export a subset.

## Filtering

The sidebar carries the same **Filters** as the radar, plus a few writer tools.
The **↺** by the Filters heading clears everything.

- **Search** — free text over names and peer references.
- **Registry Status** — On Radar, Backlog, Archive (multi-select; On Radar by
  default).
- **Visibility** *(writers)* — All, Public, or Private topics.
- **Segment**, **Rings**, **Movement** — multi-select chips.
- **Strategic relevance** — High / Medium / Low.
- **Person** — technologies linked to chosen people.
- **Completeness** — for **Factsheet** and **Peer references**, cycle each
  through *Any → Has → Missing*.
- **TRL range** and **Time to mainstream** — two-handle range sliders; the
  selected range is shown in the section label.
- **Group** — collapsible group tree; selecting a group narrows to its members.

Filters are mirrored in the URL — copy the link to share a filtered view.

## Sorting and columns

Click a column header to sort; click it again to flip the direction. Columns are
Name, Status, Ring, Segment, Movement, TRL, a summary, the peer-reference count,
and (for writers) visibility.

## Grouping by family

When technologies belong to groups, a **Group by family** button appears in the
toolbar. Turn it on to bucket rows under their top-level group, each family
collapsible; anything ungrouped sits under "Ungrouped".

## Selecting and exporting

Tick rows to build a selection — the header box selects all, and **Shift-click**
selects a range. The toolbar shows the count and a **Clear**. Use **Export** in
the top bar to download every row or just the selected ones.

## Adding a technology

Writers get a **+ Add** button in the top bar to create a new technology and
fill in its details.
