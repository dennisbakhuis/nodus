# People

People are the shared registry of owners, contributors, and experts linked to
technologies. Editing someone here updates them everywhere they're linked.

## Adding someone

Click **Add person** and fill in at least a **Full name** and **Company** (both
required). Role, department, email, and notes are optional.

## Finding and editing

- **Search** filters by name, company, role, department, or email.
- **Edit** a row to change any field.

## Linked account

The **Linked account** dropdown connects a person to an application **user**
account (one account per person). Choose "— none —" to unlink.

## Merging duplicates

**Merge** combines two records: pick the person to merge into, and the source's
technology links and account move across before it's removed. This can't be
undone.

## Deleting

**Delete** removes a person — but only once they're no longer linked to any
technology. If links remain, the app tells you how many; unlink them from those
technology cards first.
